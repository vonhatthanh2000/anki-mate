# ADR 0018: Explain speaker meaning without fact-checking

Status: Accepted

## Context

Sources may discuss science, facts, motivation, or other claims. Verifying those claims would require external research, citations, conflict handling, and a broader product purpose than transcript-based English learning.

## Decision

Briefly explain what the speaker means without independently fact-checking the source.

When the transcript does not establish truth, attribute the statement to the speaker rather than presenting it as verified fact. Keep these notes concise and include only context needed for comprehension.

## Consequences

- Lesson generation remains focused and fast.
- Meaning Overviews must distinguish summary from endorsement.
- The feature does not protect the learner from inaccurate source claims.
- External research and citation are outside v1 scope.
