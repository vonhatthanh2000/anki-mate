# ADR 0009: Exclude voice recording and pronunciation assessment

Status: Accepted

## Context

The learner's broader goal includes natural speaking, but the purpose of this feature is transcript analysis that supports understanding and later listening. Recording and evaluating speech would create a separate pronunciation and speech-assessment capability.

## Decision

Do not record the learner's voice or assess pronunciation and spoken responses.

Keep exercises text-based. The learner performs listening practice by replaying the original source independently on YouTube or TikTok after studying the transcript lesson.

## Consequences

- The feature stays centered on transcript analysis.
- No microphone permission, audio recording storage, or pronunciation scoring is required.
- Production exercises evaluate typed natural English only.
- Speaking practice is not directly measured by this feature.
