# ADR 0019: Optimize for one deep weekly lesson

Status: Accepted

## Context

The learner plans to study one video per week. Their priority is deep transcript comprehension followed by independent listening and shadowing, not high-volume content consumption or in-app speaking practice.

## Decision

Optimize Transcript Lessons for depth, clarity, and a narrow analysis workflow.

Keep self-directed replay and shadowing outside the app. Do not add voice capture, shadowing verification, streaks, scheduling, elaborate analysis editing, or version management to v1.

## Consequences

- Analysis quality matters more than throughput optimization.
- A deliberate transcript-review step is appropriate.
- Saved lesson history supports weekly revisiting without requiring habit-tracking features.
- Lightweight exercises and Anki export support the analysis but do not become the primary product experience.
